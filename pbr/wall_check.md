### Problems found in the earlier model and fixed
1. No internal walls at all on either floor — all 10 GF and 9 FF partitions added from the plan.
2. Main door (Y 18′–22′) overlapped the pooja wall — moved to Y 15′6″–19′6″, fully inside the foyer; entry steps, path and compound opening moved to line up with it.
3. First-floor slab had no stair opening and the house had no stair — dog-leg stairs GF→FF and FF→terrace added, with slab openings.
4. Mumty (12′6″–21′3″ × 2′–12′) did not sit over the stair box — rebuilt on the stair-box walls (13′0″–21′3″ × 2′0″–12′10½″), door on the north where the flight arrives.
5. Solar array sat over the skylight — moved to the north-west of the terrace, 6 panels.
6. Ground-floor stair window (13′–15′) and FF stair/lounge windows (13′–15′, 21′–26′) straddled partitions — moved to 14′–16′6″ and 22′–26′.
7. West window (14′–18′) straddled the bedroom/utility wall — split into a bedroom window (7′–11′) and a utility vent (16′–18′6″).
8. West and north toilet/utility openings had no glazing — vents glazed; master bedroom given a west window for cross-ventilation.
9. Film-only walls flanking the void blocked the gallery — replaced by balustrades on three sides.
10. Void was 9′0″ × 6′6″ against the concept's 8′1″ × 5′3″ — set to 8′1½″ × 5′3″; skylight kept inside it.
11. East balcony (4′0″ deep, behind the jali) was open at both ends, so the sit-out glazing showed through 4 ft gaps — closed with 4½″ brick return walls, full height (added 5 Nov on Punith's mark-up).
